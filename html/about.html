About Quiz App

Quiz App is a fun and interactive platform designed to challenge your knowledge across different topics. Whether you’re preparing for exams, testing your general knowledge, or just looking for a quick brain workout, Quiz App makes learning engaging and enjoyable.

Features

  • Clean and modern UI

Why Choose Us?

Quiz App is built with creativity and simplicity in mind. It’s perfect for students, professionals, or anyone who loves to learn and have fun at the same time.
